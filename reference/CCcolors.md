# Collaborative Cross colors

A vector of 8 colors for use with the mouse Collaborative Cross and
Diversity Outbreds.

## Source

\<https://csbio.unc.edu/CCstatus/index.py?run=AvailableLines.information\>

## Details

\`CCorigcolors\` (see
\<https://github.com/rqtl/qtl2/blob/main/R/CCcolors-data.R\>) are the
original eight colors for the Collaborative Cross founder strains.
\`CCcolors\` are derived from the the Okabe-Ito color blind friendly
palette in Wong (2011) Nature Methods
[doi:10.1038/nmeth.1618](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.1618) .

## Examples

``` r
data(CCcolors)
```
